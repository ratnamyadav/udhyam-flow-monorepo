## App name

UdyamFlow: Business Bookings

## Short description

Today's appointments, every booking and who has paid — for small businesses.

## Full description

UdyamFlow is the booking app for people who run their own business: clinics, tutors, salons, sports courts, therapists and trainers.

Your customers book and pay online through your own branded booking page. UdyamFlow keeps your day in your pocket.

★ YOUR DAY AT A GLANCE
• Today's appointments for each of your locations, in order
• Who is coming, for what, with whom, and whether they have paid

★ EVERY BOOKING, ONE TAP AWAY
• Browse upcoming and past bookings
• Open a booking to see the customer's contact details, the service, the time and the payment
• Mark it completed or a no-show, or cancel it, in a tap

★ BOOK IN SECONDS
• Open any business's booking link and pick a service, a person and a time
• Pay securely through Cashfree or Stripe (UPI, cards and more) and get confirmed straight away

★ MORE THAN ONE BUSINESS?
Switch between workspaces from Settings.

★ SET UP ON THE WEB
Create your workspace at udyamflow.com: pick a template for your profession, add your brand colours, locations, staff and services, connect payments, and share your booking link. Most businesses are taking bookings the same afternoon.

Free for your first 6 months.

Questions? Write to udhyamflow@nextflytech.com.

## App access instructions (for Google's reviewers)

There are no passwords: the app signs people in with their email and a 6-digit code. For the demo account, enter the email below, tap "Email me a code", then enter the code below (no email is needed for this account). Today shows the demo clinic's appointments; Bookings lists every booking (open one to mark it completed, a no-show or cancelled); Settings has workspace switching, legal links and Delete account. To book as a customer, sign out, tap "Open the booking flow" and enter the code `patel-clinic`.

- Username: `appreview@udyamflow.com`
- Password: the 6-digit sign-in code (the `REVIEW_LOGIN_CODE` value). Put it in Play Console only — don't commit it.
